### [Doses](https://github.com/IbrahimCart/dose-reminder)
**Built for my mom.** She takes about a dozen medications twice a day and kept losing track. I interviewed her, then built an iPhone app around her answers: big buttons, one-tap logging, firmer reminders when a dose is missed, and a plain-text history for her neurologist. No accounts, no server, 17 unit tests.
